## Dual Continuous Feature Modalities

This example uses real feature-film records from **`vega-datasets` (`movies.json`, BSD-3-Clause)** maintained by the University of Washington Interactive Data Lab, paired with self-hosted **Public-Domain original theatrical posters from Wikimedia Commons**. Every film carries theatrical production budgets, worldwide box-office gross, runtime, IMDb ratings, vote counts, and Rotten Tomatoes critic scores—and is projected **purely from two continuous feature vectors (`requiresTags: false`)**:

1. **Audience & Critical Reception (`4D`, weight `0.65`)**: Normalized IMDb score, Rotten Tomatoes score, log-scaled audience vote volume, and critic-vs-audience consensus delta.
2. **Production & Commercial Scale (`4D`, weight `0.35`)**: Log-scaled production budget, log-scaled worldwide gross, theatrical runtime, and return-on-investment ratio.

## Contrastive Cluster Naming & Local Subspace Re-Clustering

In historical film canons, discrete genre labels are heavily skewed—nearly four-fifths of acclaimed classics carry `"Drama"` as their primary genre, so naive majority-category naming would label five out of seven clusters *"Drama"*. Passing `features: cinemaFeatures` into `createSoftSimplexEmbeddingClusteringStrategy` evaluates **base-rate-aware z-scores and specificity lift** across continuous commercial and critical axes, producing seven pairwise-distinct archetypes (*Low-Budget Cult*, *Acclaimed Canon*, *Sleeper Hits*, *Roadshow Prestige*, *Critic Classics*, *Genre Curiosities*, and *Epic Runtime*).

Clicking any triangle or cluster badge activates **Subspace Re-Clustering Drill-Down** (`createSubspaceZoomStrategy`): SimplexViz projects out parent traits with low local variance and re-clusters covered films across **production budget**, **release era** (*Golden Age* vs. *New Hollywood*), **theatrical runtime**, **box-office ROI**, **Rotten Tomatoes consensus**, and **genre**—preserving spatial orientation via two-line `parentContextLabel` headers on the `3` outer corners.

## Chronological Cinema Timeline (1928–1976)

Use the floating timeline bar at the bottom of the canvas to **Play**, **Pause**, **Step**, or **Scrub** through `29` release-year frames spanning nearly five decades of film history—from early silent/pre-Code classics (`1928`) through post-war noir (`1944–1955`) and `1960s` roadshow epics to `1970s` New Hollywood.

Because this example configures `timeline` with `autoFinalSeed: true`, `autoFinalSeedLayout: 'adaptive'`, and `emptyClusterBehavior: 'dim'`, SimplexViz automatically pins all seven cinema archetype vertices, labels, and OKLab hues via `createFinalSeededClusteringStrategy`, anchors every film to its canonical full-corpus `(x, y)` coordinate via `createFinalSeededLayoutStrategy` (with symmetric SAT-verified adaptive scaling on sparse early decades), and renders future archetype clusters dimmed until their first film enters the timeline.
