## Dataset Provenance & Coordinate-Free Similarity Kernels

Many scientific and enterprise domains lack clean Euclidean vectors or curated hashtag vocabularies, offering instead a **domain-specific pairwise similarity function `S(a, b) ∈ [0, 1]`** (for example edit distance over biological sequences, Gower similarity across mixed boolean/categorical records, or graph-edit kernels).

This example loads all **101 named animal species** from **Richard S. Forsyth's UCI Zoo dataset** (`CC BY 4.0`)—rendered with self-hosted **Wikimedia Commons species photographs** (under Public Domain / Creative Commons licenses with full TASL attribution)—spanning mammals, birds, reptiles, fish, amphibians, insects, and marine invertebrates across 16 anatomical and ecological attributes (`hair`, `feathers`, `eggs`, `milk`, `airborne`, `aquatic`, `predator`, `toothed`, `backbone`, `breathes`, `venomous`, `fins`, `legs`, `tail`, `domestic`, `catsize`).

## How PAM k-Medoids Anchors Real Species Exemplars

- **Pairwise k-Medoids Projection & Ecological Disambiguation (`createSimilarityClusteringStrategy`)**: Rather than averaging boolean traits into synthetic centroids, **Partitioning Around Medoids (PAM)** selects *actual species* (such as *Dolphin*, *Eagle*, or *Frog*) as cluster medoids `m₁★ … m_K★` and embeds them onto adjacent 60° mesh vertices via QAP annealing. Peer-aware trait disambiguation (`features` / `disambiguatePeerClusterTitle`) distinguishes multiple mammalian or invertebrate medoids automatically (*Carnivores*, *Marine Mammals*, *Mammals · Small*, *Birds*, *Fish*, *Insects*, *Marine Fauna*).
- **Subspace Re-Clustering Over the 16 UCI Traits (`createSubspaceZoomStrategy`)**: Once you zoom into a *Mammals* or *Birds* triangle, traits that are locally constant (`σ_local = 0`, such as `milk = true` and `backbone = true` across all mammals) vanish automatically, allowing intra-class UCI attributes (`predator`, `catsize`, `aquatic`, `legs`, `domestic`, `airborne`) to re-partition the `1:4` sub-mesh while outer corners preserve their parent clade header (`CARNIVORES / Cat-Sized`).

## Reproduction Snippet

```tsx
import {
  SimplexTopicGraph,
  createAdaptiveHexMeshStrategy,
  createSimilarityClusteringStrategy,
  createOklabContrastColorStrategy,
  createCollisionDrivenLayoutStrategy,
  createSubspaceZoomStrategy,
} from '@simplex-viz/react';

<SimplexTopicGraph
  items={zooSpecies}
  meshStrategy={createAdaptiveHexMeshStrategy()}
  clusteringStrategy={createSimilarityClusteringStrategy({
    clusterCount: 7,
    getSimilarity: (a, b) => computeGowerTraitSimilarity(a.traits, b.traits),
    features: uciZoo16TraitFeatures,
  })}
  colorStrategy={createOklabContrastColorStrategy()}
  layoutStrategy={createCollisionDrivenLayoutStrategy()}
  zoomStrategy={createSubspaceZoomStrategy({
    features: uciZoo16TraitFeatures,
    getSimilarity: (a, b) => computeGowerTraitSimilarity(a.traits, b.traits),
    clusterZoomMode: 'incident-star',
  })}
  showZoomBreadcrumbs
/>;
```
