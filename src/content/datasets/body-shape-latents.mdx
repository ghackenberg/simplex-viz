## Morphological Gradients Without Species Labels

This example uses real field measurements collected by **Dr. Kristen Gorman and the Palmer Station Antarctica LTER** (`penguins.json`, released under **`CC0 1.0`**). Each specimen records exact culmen (bill) length and depth in millimeters, flipper length, body mass in grams, and island habitat (*Biscoe*, *Dream*, *Torgersen*) across *Adelie*, *Chinstrap*, and *Gentoo* populations—without passing species labels to the clustering solver (`requiresTags: false`).

## Highlighting Custom Brand Palette & Density-Adaptive Scaling

1. **Soft-Simplex Embedding Projection & Intra-Species Disambiguation (`createSoftSimplexEmbeddingClusteringStrategy`)**: Uncovers morphological archetypes purely from standardized 6D biometric measurements. Because *Adélie* and *Chinstrap* populations exhibit pronounced sexual dimorphism across culmen depth and body mass, each splits naturally into two clusters—which `features` automatically disambiguates via intra-species secondary contrast (*Adélie · Male* vs. *Adélie · Female*, *Chinstrap · Male* vs. *Chinstrap · Female*) alongside *Gentoo*.
2. **Custom Brand Palette (`createPaletteColorStrategy`)**: Demonstrates mapping a curated 6-swatch scientific publication palette (`#0891b2`, `#d97706`, `#c026d3`, `#059669`, `#2563eb`, `#e11d48`) onto mesh vertices using `'edge-contrast'` optimization while still interpolating hybrid specimens in perceptual OKLab space.
3. **Density-Adaptive Layout (`createLocalAdaptiveLayoutStrategy`)**: Fans congested specimen pockets into open covering triangles first, evaluates smooth radial crowd density within `76 px` on the dispersed coordinates, and polishes collisions locally so sparse specimens stay at full `1.0x` scale while dense species hubs taper smoothly.
4. **Subspace Re-Clustering Drill-Down (`createSubspaceZoomStrategy`)**: Zooming into a species-dominated triangle projects out the shared species baseline (`σ_local = 0`) and splits specimens locally by **island habitat** (*Biscoe*, *Dream*, *Torgersen*), **sexual dimorphism**, **body mass**, and **culmen geometry** while retaining two-line `parentContextLabel` corner plaques.
