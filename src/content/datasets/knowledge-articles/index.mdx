## Dataset Structure & Multi-Category Topology

This example loads real publication metadata harvested from the **Cornell University arXiv.org Open-Access API** (`CC0 1.0` public-domain metadata) spanning cross-disciplinary Computer Science and Mathematics papers (`cs.CG`, `cs.GR`, `cs.HC`, `cs.LG`, `cs.CV`, `cs.RO`, `cs.AI`, and `math.MG`).

Academic preprints frequently bridge two or three primary research communities—for example *Neural Radiance Fields* combining Computer Graphics (`#gr`), Computer Vision (`#cv`), and Machine Learning (`#lg`). Force-directed co-authorship or tag graphs quickly tangle into unreadable hairballs when every interdisciplinary paper connects to three or four hubs.

## Why This 5-Stage Pipeline Is Optimal Here

1. **Adaptive Hex Lattice (`createAdaptiveHexMeshStrategy`)**: Maximizes equilateral side length `s` for the current viewport aspect ratio so multi-category triangles have generous interior area.
2. **Soft-Simplex Tag Clustering (`createSoftSimplexTagClusteringStrategy`)**: Partitions subject categories into vertex topics via load-balanced QAP annealing and spreads 2- and 3-topic preprints smoothly across covering triangle interiors (`triangleSpread: 0.45`), while keeping single-category papers unconstrained in a `360°` vertex halo.
3. **Theme-Adaptive OKLab (`createThemeAdaptiveOklabColorStrategy`)**: Maintains WCAG-compliant contrast across both crisp light paper mode and dark obsidian mode while mixing interdisciplinary bridge colors in perceptual OKLab space.
4. **Collision-Local Pocket Layout (`createCollisionDrivenLayoutStrategy`)**: Leaves sparse outer categories at full `1.0x` leaf scale and shrinks only dense interdisciplinary pockets so drillable regions stand out immediately.
5. **Semantic 3→6 Tag Drill-Down (`createSemanticZoomStrategy`)**: Clicking any subdivided interdisciplinary triangle promotes vertex-exclusive secondary arXiv categories (`stat.ML`, `math.MG`, `cs.IR`, `cs.DC`) ahead of shared parent categories, pairing refined outer corners with two-line `parentContextLabel` headers while guaranteeing `6` pairwise-distinct sub-vertex badges.
