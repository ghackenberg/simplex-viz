## Vocal-Tract Acoustic Vectors

This example loads real acoustic feature vectors from **David Deterding's Spoken Vowel Recognition dataset** (`Connectionist Bench`, **UCI Machine Learning Repository**, `CC BY 4.0`). Speakers uttered steady-state British English monophthongs (`i:`, `ɪ`, `ɛ`, `æ`, `ɑ:`, `ɒ`, `ɔ:`, `ʊ`, `u:`, `ʌ`, `ɜ:`), digitized with LPC reflection coefficients transformed into **10 log-area vocal-tract cross-section parameters** and rendered as SVG LPC envelope capsules (`SvgNodeView`).

## Why Fixed Lattice + Planar NNLS + Uniform PBD Fit Acoustic Calibration

1. **Fixed Grid Lattice (`createFixedLatticeMeshStrategy({ cols: 3, rows: 2 })`)**: Locks an invariant `6`-vertex vowel-formant reference chart across screen sizes so phonetic quadrants stay spatially anchored.
2. **Planar NNLS Embedding Projection & Register Disambiguation (`createEmbeddingClusteringStrategy`)**: Evaluates every planar mesh triangle `τ = (μ₁, μ₂, μ₃)` and solves exact Non-Negative Least Squares (`wᵢ ≥ 0`, `∑ wᵢ = 1`) so intermediate vowel articulations land at their minimum-residual acoustic convex combination, while `features` qualifies overlapping formant hubs by speaker vocal-tract register (*Female* vs. *Male*).
3. **Uniform PBD Layout (`createPbdUniformLayoutStrategy`)**: Disperses co-located phoneme capsules into the open interior of their covering vowel triangles while applying a single global scale factor `α` so capsule widths remain strictly proportional across the entire vowel chart with `0.0 px` overlap.
4. **Subspace Re-Clustering Drill-Down (`createSubspaceZoomStrategy`)**: Drilling into a broad vowel quadrant separates individual monophthong phonemes (`i:`, `ɪ`, `ɛ`, `æ`, …), speaker vocal-tract register, fundamental pitch (`F0`), and local 10D LPC residual axes while keeping parent vowel anchors visible on the `3` outer corners.
