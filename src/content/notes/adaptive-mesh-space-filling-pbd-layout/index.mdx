When dozens of items attach to one topic anchor while adjacent triangles sit nearly empty, naive uniform scaling shrinks the **entire** graph down just to resolve one crowded pocket. Even well-separated nodes on the opposite side of the canvas become unreadable—a failure mode we call *collateral shrink*.

## Fitting the 60° Lattice to Real Viewports

Before placing a single item, Stage 1 (`createAdaptiveHexMeshStrategy` and `buildSimplexMesh`) evaluates the container's `(width, height)` aspect ratio:

- **Orientation Selection**: Landscape and ultrawide canvases favor `horizontal` triangle rows; tall mobile viewports automatically rotate to `vertical` columns with optimal `wideFirst` parity.
- **Adaptive Padding (`resolveDefaultInnerPadding`)**: Margins scale smoothly between `32 px` and `48 px` with viewport size, letting equilateral triangle edges grow past `minSideLength: 130` to fill the available frame rather than clustering in the center.

## Fanning Pockets Into Open Triangle Interiors

Instead of shrinking every node immediately upon collision, Stage 4 separates spatial relaxation from scale selection using composable **`LayoutSeeder × PositionRelaxer × ScaleController`** pipelines:

1. **Inward-Fan Seeding (`createMeshInwardFanSeeder`)**: Co-located anchors along shared edges or vertices are fanned deliberately toward the open interior of their covering simplex triangles rather than stacking on a single point.
2. **Space-Filling PBD (`createSpaceFillingPbdRelaxer`)**: Iterative Position-Based Dynamics + SAT projection disperses dense clusters outward within triangle boundaries (`covering-triangle containment`), leaving well-spaced single-cluster halos unconstrained.
3. **Localized Scale Control**: After dispersion settles, `createDensityPriorScaleController` (`createLocalAdaptiveLayoutStrategy`) computes smooth radial crowd gradients (`crowdRadiusPx: 76`, `minLocalScale: 0.35`), or `createCollisionLocalScaleController` (`createCollisionDrivenLayoutStrategy`) shrinks only pockets still experiencing local hull contact—leaving sparse items at a crisp `1.0x`.

Whether you pick `createPbdUniformLayoutStrategy` for strict cross-corpus size comparability or `createLocalAdaptiveLayoutStrategy` for maximum legibility in skewed real-world distributions, every solved frame preserves `overlapCount === 0` and strict triangle containment.
