Spring-electric force graphs treat node overlap as a soft penalty: increase repulsion and related nodes fly apart; increase attraction and text cards collide into unreadable stacks.

## Exact Convex Hulls + Spatial Hash SAT

SimplexViz treats **covering-triangle containment** and **zero convex overlap** as hard geometric invariants rather than soft forces:

- Renderers measure every node—whether a 10 px circle, a 14-bar waveform capsule, or a 110×28 px React JSX card—into an exact `ConvexGeometry` (`ConvexCircle` or `ConvexPolygon`).
- `UniformSpatialHashGrid` prunes broad-phase pair checks to `O(N)` expected time per iteration.
- Narrow-phase **Separating Axis Theorem (SAT)** computes exact Minimum Translation Vectors (`computeConvexMtv`, `computeCirclePolygonMtv`), clamping every node strictly inside its covering simplex envelope until `maxPenetrationPx === 0`.
