## Key UX & Geometric Mechanics in `drill-down` Mode

1. **Local Subspace Variance Projection**: When using `createSubspaceZoomStrategy`, attributes that defined the parent cluster (such as `milk = true` inside *Mammals*) have zero intra-subset variance and vanish automatically, letting secondary domain attributes, continuous embeddings, or similarity landmarks separate the zoomed subset cleanly across the `6` sub-vertices.
2. **3-Tier Spatial Orientation & Collision-Free Sub-Vertex Badges**: Every zoomed `1:4` frame preserves spatial orientation via three coordinated cues: (a) **two-line corner plaques** (`parentContextLabel` above `subclusterLabel`) whenever an outer corner is locally refined—while unchanged outer corners remain clean single-line primary capsules without `PARENT / Parent` stutter; (b) **explicit `A ↔ B` midpoint bridge plaques** (chamfered hexagonal geometry distinguishing `role: 'intermediate'` from primary capsules) with canonical uniqueness across all `6` sub-vertices; and (c) **color-pip breadcrumb trails** (`ZoomBreadcrumbSegment[]`) displaying the active parent triangle's three corner hues.
3. **Direct Internal `1:4` Subdivision Preview**: Any triangle with `isDrillable === true` renders its 3 internal midpoint-to-midpoint edges using your active `SvgEdgeView` (`DashedMeshEdgeSvgView`, `SolidMeshEdgeSvgView`, or custom), signaling subdivision affordance at a glance.
4. **Content Pass-Through vs. Single-Triangle Leaf Focus**: When `stopWhenFullScale: true` (default), triangles stop `1:4` subdividing as soon as all covered items reach `fullScaleThreshold` (`0.995`). While a triangle is still `1:4` drillable, content nodes inside it set `isPassThrough: true` so clicking anywhere inside triggers `1:4` subdivision. Once leaf scale is reached (`leafTriangleFocus: true`, default), content nodes inside the leaf triangle become directly clickable, while clicking the **free triangle face** focuses that single un-subdivided triangle (`kind: 'triangle-focus'`) across the viewport—and clicking its free face again toggles back out (`drillUp`).
5. **Uniform 1-Ring Cluster & Subcluster Focus with Simplex-Distance Dimming**: In `'incident-star'` mode (default), every cluster and subcluster badge—at any zoom depth, including `1.0×` leaf triangles and sparse corners—behaves identically: clicking it focuses its lattice-conformal 1-ring star of incident triangles and dims active items by barycentric simplex distance `d = 1 − w̃₀ ∈ [0, 1]` (`opacity = 0.32 + 0.68 · w̃₀`). Clicking a rim neighbor cluster inside an active star (or any of the 3 corners of a `'triangle-focus'` frame) switches laterally at the same breadcrumb depth to its full 1-ring star, while clicking the focused center cluster again toggles cleanly back up one level (`drillUp`).
6. **Dimmed Neighborhood Context & Lateral Switching**: Surrounding triangles and clusters are projected around the focused sub-mesh via `ZoomAffineTransform` at reduced opacity without node hover distraction. Clicking any adjacent dimmed neighbor triangle switches laterally at the same zoom depth—subdividing `1:4` when dense or focusing as a single un-subdivided `'triangle-focus'` when already at leaf scale.
