Stage 2 (`SimplexClusteringStrategy`) solves three coupled mathematical problems:

1. **Partitioning**: Grouping domain items into `K` coherent topic clusters or medoids/centroids.
2. **Planar Mesh Embedding (QAP)**: Assigning those `K` clusters to the `K` vertices of the 60° simplex mesh so pairwise cluster affinity aligns with 1-hop mesh adjacency (preserving identities across updates via the **Hungarian algorithm**).
3. **Barycentric Simplex Anchoring**: Mapping every item onto its covering vertex (`1`-simplex), shared edge (`2`-simplex), or triangle face (`3`-simplex) with normalized weights `∑ w_i = 1`.

## Tag Rules: Planar Hitting-Set & Explaining-Away

In tagged collections, two subtle edge cases arise when projecting hypergraph tag overlaps onto a 2D planar triangulation:

- **`sharedTagRule: 'planar-hitting-set'` *(Default)***: If a bridge tag co-occurs across clusters that cannot all touch on a planar graph, a weighted hitting-set pass promotes minimal bridge coverage onto shared mesh edges so cross-cutting items remain geometrically faithfully tethered.
- **`anchorRule: 'explaining-away'` *(Default)***: Ranks incident clusters by specificity and prunes redundant secondary matches once an item's tags are explained, preventing generic tags from dragging every node toward the canvas center.

## Closed-Form Planar NNLS Projection for Embeddings

For untagged vector embeddings `x ∈ ℝ^d`, `createEmbeddingClusteringStrategy` evaluates every candidate vertex, mesh edge, and mesh triangle `τ = (c₁, c₂, c₃)` and solves the exact constrained least-squares projection onto the 2-simplex `Δ²`:

```text
minimize   || x − (w₁·μ₁ + w₂·μ₂ + w₃·μ₃) ||₂²
subject to w₁, w₂, w₃ ≥ 0  and  w₁ + w₂ + w₃ = 1
```

Selecting the planar simplex `τ*` with minimal reconstruction residual guarantees that every embedded item lands inside a true planar mesh triangle rather than across non-adjacent clusters.

## Base-Rate-Aware Discriminative Cluster Labeling (`features` & `peerClusters`)

When clustering untagged embeddings or pairwise similarity kernels, naively naming each cluster after its most frequent category often produces duplicate badges whenever a single class dominates the corpus (for example, `77%` of classic feature films carrying `"Drama"` as a primary genre, or two penguin clusters sharing the same species).

SimplexViz solves this directly inside Stage 2 and exposes the underlying primitives for custom `deriveClusterMeta` callbacks:

1. **Built-In `features` Option on Embedding & Similarity Strategies**: Passing `features: SubspaceZoomFeature<TItem>[]` to `createSoftSimplexEmbeddingClusteringStrategy`, `createEmbeddingClusteringStrategy`, or `createSimilarityClusteringStrategy` invokes `assignDiscriminativeClusterLabels` across all `K` sibling clusters—scoring numeric features by standardized z-score separation (`|z_k|`), boolean/categorical facets by specificity lift above corpus base rates, and shared taxonomic groups by intra-group secondary contrast (such as *Adélie · Male* vs. *Adélie · Female*).
2. **Sibling Context in `deriveClusterMeta`**: Every `ClusterDerivationContext<TItem, TTagMeta>` receives `allItems` and `peerClusters: readonly PeerClusterContext<TItem, TTagMeta>[]`, enabling custom domain meta builders to call `resolveDiscriminativeClusterLabel(ctx, { features })` or `disambiguatePeerClusterTitle(ctx, getBaseTitle, getQualifier)` with guaranteed pairwise uniqueness across the mesh.
