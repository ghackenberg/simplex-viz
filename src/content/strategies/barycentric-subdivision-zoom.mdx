## Universal Conformal 1:4 Equilateral Subdivision

Clicking any drillable triangle splits its edges at midpoints `(M_ab, M_bc, M_ca)` into `4` congruent equilateral sub-triangles (`3` corner triangles matching parent orientation + `1` inverted center triangle).

To preserve spatial orientation at every zoom depth without cluttering the sub-mesh:

1. **Unchanged Outer Corners (`V₀, V₁, V₂`)**: Retain their primary cluster capsule and exact parent title, or switch to a two-line plaque with `parentContextLabel` when **Adaptive Contrast Stretch** shifts an outer corner's weight profile.
2. **Explicit Midpoint Bridge Plaques (`M₀₁, M₁₂, M₂₀`)**: Render as compact chamfered hexagonal sub-cluster plaques (`role: 'intermediate'`) labeled with their immediate edge bridge relation (`A ↔ B`), blended in perceptual OKLab space.
3. **Uniform 1-Ring Cluster & Subcluster Stars with Simplex-Distance Dimming**: In `'incident-star'` mode (default), every cluster and subcluster badge—at any depth, including `1.0×` leaf frames and sparse corners—focuses strictly its incident lattice triangles (never spawning off-grid spotlight triangles) and dims active items continuously by barycentric simplex distance `d = 1 − w̃₀ ∈ [0, 1]` (`opacity = 0.32 + 0.68 · w̃₀`). Clicking a rim cluster switches laterally at the same depth, while clicking the focused center cluster toggles back up (`drillUp`).
4. **Single-Triangle Leaf Focus (`'triangle-focus'`)**: Once a triangle reaches leaf scale (`leafTriangleFocus: true`), its content items become directly clickable without pointer pass-through, while clicking the free triangle background focuses that single un-subdivided triangle across the viewport (clicking its free face again toggles back out).
5. **Color-Coded Breadcrumb Segments**: Every drill step emits `ZoomBreadcrumbSegment[]` carrying the parent corner names and hex swatches so users always know where they are in the global atlas.

## Usage Example

```tsx
import {
  SimplexTopicGraph,
  createCollisionDrivenLayoutStrategy,
  createBarycentricZoomStrategy,
} from '@simplex-viz/react';

<SimplexTopicGraph
  items={items}
  layoutStrategy={createCollisionDrivenLayoutStrategy()}
  zoomStrategy={createBarycentricZoomStrategy({
    clusterZoomMode: 'incident-star',
    adaptiveContrastStretch: true,
  })}
  showZoomBreadcrumbs
/>;
```
