## Brand Tokens Meet Perceptual OKLab Blending

**Custom Brand Palette** converts your supplied sRGB hex colors into OKLab tuples, assigns them onto the 60° mesh to maximize perceptual contrast across adjacent triangle edges (`mode: 'edge-contrast'`), and computes `mixedColorHex` for multi-cluster items in OKLab space so even custom corporate palettes produce clean, legible intermediate blends.

## Usage Example

```tsx
import {
  SimplexTopicGraph,
  createPaletteColorStrategy,
} from '@simplex-viz/react';

const brandColors = createPaletteColorStrategy(
  ['#0891b2', '#d97706', '#c026d3', '#059669', '#2563eb', '#e11d48'],
  { mode: 'edge-contrast' },
);

<SimplexTopicGraph items={items} colorStrategy={brandColors} />;
```
