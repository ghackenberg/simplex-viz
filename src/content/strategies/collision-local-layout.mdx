## Local Pocket Shrinking + 1:4 Drill-Down Synergy

In real-world corpora, one interdisciplinary triangle may hold 25 items while neighboring triangles hold 3. Instead of shrinking the entire atlas to fit the busiest triangle, **Collision-Local Pocket Layout** composes `createMeshInwardFanSeeder()`, `createSpaceFillingPbdRelaxer()`, and `createCollisionLocalScaleController()`: sparse regions stay at crisp `1.0x` scale, while dense pockets pack cleanly and automatically mark their parent triangle as `isDrillable: true` for `1:4` conformal zoom!

## Usage Example

```tsx
import {
  SimplexTopicGraph,
  createCollisionDrivenLayoutStrategy,
  createBarycentricZoomStrategy,
} from '@simplex-viz/react';

<SimplexTopicGraph
  items={items}
  layoutStrategy={createCollisionDrivenLayoutStrategy({ minLocalScale: 0.3 })}
  zoomStrategy={createBarycentricZoomStrategy()}
/>;
```
